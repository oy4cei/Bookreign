Privacy policy

A personal library on your device, with optional online catalog and cover searches.

Effective 4 October 2026 · Version 1.0.0

Data on your device

Books, notes, cover photos, storage locations and lending history are stored on this device. You enter borrowers’ names yourself; the app does not read your contacts. There are no accounts or synchronization between devices.

The app contains no ads, usage analytics or tracking. It has no built-in mechanism for sending your library to the developer.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to scan barcodes and photograph covers. The app receives only the images you select from your photo library. Apple Vision recognizes text on the device; photos are not uploaded for recognition. Recognized ISBNs or text may be sent to a catalog when you search.

Catalog searches

Open Library receives your ISBN or search query. ISBN lookups may also send the query to an additional book catalog. Searching for another cover sends an ISBN, title or author to a catalog. These services also receive technical connection data, such as your IP address.

Covers and external sites

Linked covers are loaded from image hosts, including covers.openlibrary.org and sites returned by a catalog. Image search opens Google with your query. Importing an HTTPS image URL requests the selected site directly and may follow redirects. These sites receive the request and technical connection data.

Selected photos and confirmed alternative covers are saved locally. Covers stored only as links may be downloaded when a book is displayed.

Backups and deletion

You choose where to save a JSON backup or CSV file using Files. Backups may contain notes, borrowers’ names and photos. If you select cloud storage, its provider will handle the file. The queue of ISBNs not yet added to the library is not included in the JSON backup.

Data stays on the device until you delete it in the app or delete the app itself. Exported files and system backups are managed separately. Offloading the app while keeping its data does not delete the library. Restoring a JSON backup replaces the current library after confirmation.

Your choices

Manual entry and your saved library work without internet access. You can change camera permission in iOS Settings. External catalogs, websites and file services handle requests under their own policies; the app does not determine their data retention periods.

The library uses local SQLite storage. Interface settings and the pending scanning queue are also stored locally. Device backups may contain app data, depending on your iOS and backup-provider settings. Manage those backups separately.

About this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. These page files contain no analytics, advertising, third-party fonts, embedded remote images or JavaScript. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security. GitHub handles hosting logs under its Privacy Statement.

Support messages

When you email support, Ievgen Tsvietkov receives your email address, message content and any files you voluntarily attach through Gmail. These details are used to answer your request and provide support. Messages remain in the developer’s mailbox until deleted; you can request deletion by emailing oy4cei@gmail.com. Google’s handling and retention of email data follow its Privacy Policy.
