Bookreign 1.1.0 (13) catalogs physical books on iPhone and iPad, iOS 17+. Local use requires no sign-in, subscription or in-app purchase. First launch opens an empty library.

Local review path:
1. Tap Add book to open the scanner (real device and camera permission required). Other ways offers manual entry, catalog search and cover text recognition.
2. Manually add a fictional book and choose a storage location. Edit its details or select a cover photo. Move or lend the copy, then mark its return under Places > On loan.
3. Filter by author or genre in Topics. In Settings change language/theme, view the privacy policy or export JSON/CSV. Full restore and Undo are available only in local mode.

Family sharing review:
1. Sign into iCloud in iOS Settings on two devices with different Apple accounts. No Bookreign credentials or developer test account are needed.
2. In Bookreign > Settings > Family sharing, enable access and confirm the explanation that all books, notes, locations, cover photos and borrower names will be shared. Select people using Apple's sharing sheet; public access is disabled.
3. Open the invitation on the second device. A writer can merge their entire library; a reader must save their previous library to Files before replacing it. Cancellation preserves the previous library.
4. Add or edit a sample book. Use Refresh now on the other device. Offline changes are retained; after restarting, editing waits for verification of the original iCloud account. Read/export remain available.
5. Manage permissions or stop sharing using Apple's sheet. Revoked participants retain a read-only, exportable copy and may delete it locally. The owner retains their library.

The owner supplies iCloud storage quota. Sync sends a compressed library snapshot with per-field last-edit timestamps. No developer server, ads or usage analytics. Catalog lookup sends ISBN/title queries to Open Library and may use an additional Ukrainian catalog. Cover requests go to image hosts; Google image search opens in the browser. OCR uses Apple Vision on device. There are no loan reminder notifications or ebook reading. Contacts are not read. Ordinary release UI is sufficient; no launch arguments or fixtures are required.
